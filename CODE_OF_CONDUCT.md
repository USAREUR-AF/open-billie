# Code of Conduct

Open Billie welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds,
experience levels, and viewpoints. Contributors are expected to be respectful,
constructive, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Use considerate language and assume good faith while discussing technical
  disagreements.
- Accept constructive feedback and focus criticism on ideas and work, not
  people.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish personal information, credentials, private
  sensor data, or deployment details without authorization.
- Follow the project's contribution, security-reporting, and data-boundary
  guidance.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, personal attacks, deliberate
disruption, or publishing private information without consent are not
acceptable in project spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers. Do not use a
public issue for sensitive reports. Before opening public contributions, the
repository maintainers must publish a reachable private contact route in the
repository profile and link it here. Maintainers will review reports and may
remove content or restrict participation when needed to protect project
participants.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
spaces where a person represents the project.
